About Me

Hello, and welcome to my e-portfolio! My name is Constance Malacarne, but I go by Connie. I am based in Athens, Georgia, and I am currently an undergraduate senior at the University of Georgia studying Health Promotion. Throughout my time there, I have maintained the Zell Miller Scholarship.

As a child, I was particularly interested in the field of medicine, but my interests shifted toward public health as I began to question why healthcare is not always sought out or accessible when it is needed. Consequently, I am especially interested in examining disparities in healthcare access and outcomes. My academic experiences have driven me to better understand the structural and social barriers that shape inequities in care, which I have also observed firsthand while working in various community health settings.

I am currently in the process of applying to graduate programs to pursue a Master of Public Health in Health Policy and Management. Coming from an under-resourced community, my long-term goal is to contribute to the assessment, design, and evaluation of policies that address persistent community health challenges and improve access to care.
