About Me

Background, academic journey, and developing interests in health policy and equity.

University of Georgia | Health Promotion | Zell Miller Scholar

My name is Constance Malacarne, and I am a senior at the University of Georgia studying Health Promotion, based in Athens, Georgia. Throughout my undergraduate career, I have maintained the Zell Miller Scholarship while developing a strong academic foundation in public health.

My initial interest in healthcare began with a focus on medicine, but over time, my perspective shifted toward public health. I became increasingly interested in understanding why healthcare is not always accessible or utilized when needed. This shift led me to explore broader systemic and structural factors that influence health outcomes, particularly among underserved populations.

Public Health Focus

I am particularly interested in examining disparities in healthcare access and outcomes. My academic experiences have allowed me to analyze the social and structural barriers that contribute to inequities in care, with a focus on how policy, environment, and access intersect to shape health outcomes.

Professional Goals

I am currently applying to graduate programs to pursue a Master of Public Health in Health Policy and Management. My long-term goal is to contribute to the development and implementation of policies that improve access to care and reduce health disparities across diverse populations.
