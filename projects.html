Projects

Selected academic work demonstrating research, analysis, and public health application.

Program Plan

Developed a comprehensive public health program plan addressing a targeted health issue, incorporating needs assessment, goal setting, and evidence-based intervention strategies. The project emphasized practical application of program planning frameworks and the development of measurable outcomes to improve community health.

Evaluation Project

Designed an evaluation framework to assess the effectiveness of a public health intervention. This project involved identifying key performance indicators, selecting appropriate evaluation methods, and analyzing outcomes to determine program impact and areas for improvement.

Literature Review: Breast Cancer Disparities

Authored a 25+ page literature review analyzing racial and ethnic disparities in breast cancer diagnosis across the United States. Synthesized findings from 20 peer-reviewed studies to examine how socioeconomic, geographic, and healthcare system factors contribute to delayed diagnosis among minority populations.

Technical Manual: Type II Diabetes Education

Collaborated with a team to develop a patient-centered educational manual on Type 2 diabetes, covering prevention, management, nutrition, and mental health considerations. Designed content to be accessible and actionable, translating complex health information into practical guidance for diverse audiences.
